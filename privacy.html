Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This website is operated by LC Technologies, Lda., based in Eixo, Aveiro, Portugal. We design and manufacture custom RF, microwave and millimeter-wave instrumentation for research institutions. For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us at info@cupidotech.com. We collect personal data only when you voluntarily submit the contact form on this website. The fields collected are: full name, email address, organisation or company name (optional), and subject and message content. We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics platforms. The only passive data collected from visitors is aggregated pageview counts via Flag Counter, which records visit statistics without identifying individual users. The sole purpose of collecting contact form data is to respond to your enquiry. We do not use it for marketing, do not add you to mailing lists, and do not process it for any purpose other than direct correspondence with you. The legal basis for processing is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), specifically responding to a communication you initiated. Enquiry data is retained only for as long as necessary to handle the correspondence. If no business relationship results from the contact, data is deleted within 12 months. If a project or contract follows, data is retained for the duration of that relationship and for the legally required period thereafter. Your data is accessible only to LC Technologies personnel directly involved in responding to your enquiry. We do not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties. The contact form submission is processed via our own server infrastructure. No third-party form processing services are used. Under GDPR, you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your data, object to processing, and lodge a complaint with the CNPD (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados) at cnpd.pt. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@cupidotech.com. We will respond within 30 days. This website contains links to external sites including institutional partners, research programmes, and EU funding bodies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and recommend reviewing their respective policies. If we make material changes to this policy, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
