At the Edge

LC Technologies has contributed instrumentation, sub-assemblies and complete systems to leading research institutions across Europe and beyond, from fusion reactors to orbital observatories and next-generation radio telescopes.

Customers & Partners
ESA · CubeSat Programme
W-Cube

W-Cube is an ESA CubeSat mission designed to perform W-band (75 GHz) atmospheric channel propagation measurements from a LEO nanosatellite. LC Technologies contributed to the design and development of the beacon receiver front-end for the ground station measurement terminal.

SKA Observatory · International
Square Kilometre Array

The SKAO is a next-generation radio astronomy-driven Big Data facility that will revolutionise our understanding of the Universe and the laws of fundamental physics. Enabled by cutting-edge technology, it promises to have a major impact on society, in science and beyond.

EU Funded Research · PRR

Recuperar Portugal

LC Technologies participates in European co-funded research under the Recovery and Resilience Plan, supporting the development of advanced millimeter-wave diagnostic instrumentation for nuclear fusion.
